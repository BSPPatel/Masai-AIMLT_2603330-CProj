Zepto Order Cancellation Policy

1. Instant Cancellation Window
Because Zepto begins packing within 60 seconds of order placement, free cancellations are permitted only within 60 seconds from the moment payment is confirmed.

2. Post-Packing Cancellation Charges
- If an order is cancelled after packing has started but before dispatch, a nominal restocking fee of Rs. 30 may be deducted.
- Once the order is dispatched and out for delivery, cancellations are not permitted through self-service and will be subject to a 100% cancellation charge unless there is a verified delay exceeding 30 minutes from the promised delivery window.

3. Merchant-Initiated Cancellations
If an item is out of stock at the dark store during packing, Zepto will automatically cancel the missing item and process a full refund to the original payment source immediately.
