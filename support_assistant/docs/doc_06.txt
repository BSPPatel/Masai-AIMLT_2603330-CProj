Zepto Damaged or Missing Items Policy

1. Reporting Timeframe
Customers must inspect all delivered packages promptly. Any damaged, defective, tampered, expired, or missing items must be reported within 24 hours of delivery through the 'Order Help' section of the Zepto application.

2. Evidence Requirements
For damaged or expired goods, customers are required to upload clear photographs showing:
- The affected item with the damage clearly visible.
- The packaging and batch label or expiry date stamp.
Missing item claims are cross-referenced with dark store picking barcodes and automated bag weight logs.

3. Resolution Options
Upon claim verification, customers are offered:
- Immediate 100% refund to original payment method or instant Zepto credits.
- Free express re-delivery of the missing item within 15 minutes where inventory allows.
