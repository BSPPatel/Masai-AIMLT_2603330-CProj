Zepto Customer Support Hours Policy

1. Operating Hours
Zepto Customer Support operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year (24/7). Customer assistance is active even on public holidays and festival days.

2. Support Channels
- In-App Live Chat: Available 24/7 with an average first-response resolution time under 60 seconds.
- Voice Support Helpline: Operational from 6:00 AM to 1:00 AM daily for urgent delivery assistance.
- Email Support: Reach out to support@zepto.com for business queries, formal grievances, and bulk order requests (response turnaround within 12 hours).

3. Escalations
For unresolved issues, customers can escalate to the Customer Grievance Officer via the in-app escalation form or email grievance@zepto.com, with resolutions committed within 24 to 48 business hours.
