Zepto Gift Cards Policy

1. Purchase and Denominations
Zepto electronic gift cards can be purchased in denominations ranging from Rs. 250 to Rs. 10,000 via the Zepto app or authorized partner platforms. Gift cards are delivered instantly via SMS and registered email address.

2. Redemption Rules
- Gift cards can be redeemed against any grocery or fresh produce purchase on Zepto.
- The 16-digit gift card code and 6-digit PIN can be added to the customer's Zepto Wallet balance under 'Payments'.
- Gift card balances can be used in combination with other payment methods (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards).

3. Validity and Restrictions
- All Zepto gift cards have a validity period of 12 months from the date of issuance.
- Unused balances cannot be transferred to bank accounts or redeemed for cash.
- Expired gift cards cannot be revalidated or refunded.
