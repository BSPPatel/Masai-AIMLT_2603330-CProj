Zepto Delivery Policy

1. Delivery Timelines
Zepto delivers everyday groceries, fresh produce, and household essentials in 10 minutes to select serviceable pin codes across major metro cities in India. Delivery hours operate between 6:00 AM and 2:00 AM daily.

2. Delivery Charges
- Standard delivery fee is Rs. 15 for orders below Rs. 149.
- Orders of Rs. 149 or above qualify for free delivery for all standard customers.
- Zepto Pass members receive unlimited free delivery on all eligible orders above Rs. 99.
- A nominal surge or weather fee of Rs. 10 to Rs. 25 may apply during extreme weather events, heavy monsoon showers, or peak holiday demand periods.

3. Delivery Process and Safety
Our delivery partners follow contactless delivery upon customer request. If a customer is unreachable via phone or doorbell for more than 5 minutes after arrival, the order may be returned to the dark store and marked as undelivered.
