Zepto Membership Tiers Policy (Zepto Pass)

1. Subscription Plans
Zepto Pass offers subscription tiers designed for frequent shoppers:
- Monthly Plan: Rs. 99 per month.
- Quarterly Plan: Rs. 249 for 3 months.
- Annual Plan: Rs. 799 per year.

2. Membership Benefits
- Unlimited Free Deliveries on orders above Rs. 99 across all operational dark stores.
- Extra 5% to 20% discount on select high-frequency essentials including milk, bread, vegetables, and snacks.
- Waiver of surge pricing during high-demand or rainy periods up to 3 orders per month.
- Priority customer support queue with sub-30 second live agent response times.

3. Cancellation and Renewal
Memberships renew automatically on the billing date. Users may cancel anytime from the account settings tab before the renewal date without penalty.
