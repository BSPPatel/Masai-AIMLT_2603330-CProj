Zepto Returns & Refunds Policy

1. Return Window and Eligibility
Due to the perishable nature of groceries, dairy, and fresh produce, items can only be returned at the doorstep during handover. For packaged FMCG items, personal care, and dry goods, return requests can be submitted via the app within 24 hours of delivery.

2. Refund Initiation and Processing Times
- Refunds for doorstep rejections are initiated immediately.
- UPI and Zepto Wallet refunds reflect within 2 to 4 hours.
- Credit Card, Debit Card, and Net Banking refunds take 3 to 5 business days depending on the issuing bank.

3. Non-Returnable Categories
Fresh dairy packets once opened, cut fruits, meat and seafood, innerwear, and hygiene-sensitive items cannot be returned unless received in a spoiled or expired condition.
